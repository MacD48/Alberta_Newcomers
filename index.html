Quick Start Checklist

  • 1. Settlement agency — Contact the main immigrant-serving organization in your city within days of arrival.
  • 2. Health card — Apply for AHCIP; use Health Link 811 for advice while waiting.
  • 3. Bank account — Open a newcomer package at a major bank or ATB/Servus (no Canadian credit history required).
  • 4. SIN — Apply for a Social Insurance Number at Service Canada if you plan to work.
  • 5. School registration — Register children with the local public or Catholic school board.
  • 6. Transit — Get a local transit app/card and learn routes.
  • 7. Housing — Search private rentals and apply early for subsidized housing waitlists.
  • 8. Community — Connect with places of worship, cultural associations, and ethnic grocery stores.
