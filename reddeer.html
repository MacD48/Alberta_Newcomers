Red Deer is smaller than Edmonton and Calgary, so some specialized ethnic stores and services are more limited. Settlement agencies are the best first contact — they know current local options and can connect you with community groups. Many residents also travel to Edmonton or Calgary for certain products.

Settlement Services

Catholic Social Services – Immigration & Settlement

Main settlement provider for Red Deer and area. Supports for temporary residents, refugees, and others.

Address area: 5000 Gaetz Avenue

Healthcare

  • Alberta Find a Provider — Search by Red Deer / Central Zone
  • AHCIP registration
  • Health Link 811 · Emergency 911
  • Red Deer Regional Hospital is the main hospital; settlement workers can help navigate clinics

Transit

Red Deer Transit — bus service across the city. MyBus for real-time information. Action Bus for accessible / paratransit service.

Red Deer Transit Map: Transit

Inter-city buses to Edmonton and Calgary: Ebus, Red Arrow, and others (see 511 Alberta).

Groceries & Ethnic Markets

Selection is more limited than in Edmonton or Calgary. Major chains (Superstore, Walmart, Safeway/Sobeys) carry growing international sections.

  • Search Google Maps for “African grocery Red Deer”, “Asian supermarket Red Deer”, or “international food Red Deer” for the most current small stores
  • Many newcomers order online or travel to Edmonton/Calgary for specialty African or Asian ingredients
  • Settlement agencies and cultural associations sometimes organize group orders or share supplier information
Map: Grocery Stores

Banks

All major Canadian banks have branches in Red Deer, plus ATB Financial and Servus Credit Union. Newcomer packages are available — ask at any branch.

Map: Banks

Housing

  • Red Deer Housing Authority — Community housing units and rent supplement programs (apply online)
  • Private rentals are generally more affordable than in Calgary or Edmonton
  • CSS / CARE can assist with housing search, especially for refugees and high-need newcomers
  • Also check Metis Urban Housing and other specialized providers if applicable

Schools

  • Red Deer Public Schools
  • Red Deer Catholic Regional Schools
  • CARE / settlement workers provide Settlement Support in Schools to help newcomer students and families transition
  • Red Deer Polytechnic — post-secondary options and language upgrading

Places of Worship

  • Red Deer Islamic Centre / Salah El Deen Mosque — 195 Douglas Avenue
  • Christian churches of many denominations (including some with newcomer-focused ministries)
  • Other faith communities exist; ask settlement workers or the library for current contacts
  • For larger mosques, temples, or cultural events, Edmonton and Calgary are within a reasonable drive
Map: Places of Worship

Parks & Recreation

Waskasoo Park system — over 100 km of trails along the river valley for walking, cycling, and winter activities. City recreation facilities for swimming, skating, fitness, and arts.

Red Deer Recreation Map: Waskasoo Park

Additional Local Tips

  • Red Deer Public Library — ESL resources, conversation circles, digital literacy, and community information
  • Dial 211 for community and social service referrals
  • Local Immigration Partnership and neighbourhood community associations help with integration and events
