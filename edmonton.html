Banks (Newcomer Packages)

Major banks offer newcomer programs (fee waivers 12–24 months, no Canadian credit history required). Alberta options: ATB Financial and Servus Credit Union.

  • RBC, TD, Scotiabank, BMO, CIBC — Newcomer / New to Canada packages
  • ATB Financial (Alberta-owned) · Servus Credit Union
Map: Banks in Edmonton

Housing

  • Civida — Largest affordable / community housing provider in Edmonton (apply for community housing & rent assistance)
  • Private rentals: Rentals.ca, RentFaster, Kijiji (use caution), Zumper
  • Settlement agencies can help with applications and temporary housing for eligible refugees

Schools

  • Edmonton Public Schools (EPSB) — Welcome Centre for newcomer intake & registration
  • Edmonton Catholic Schools (ECSD)
  • Documents typically needed: child’s birth certificate/passport, immigration documents, proof of address, immunization records

Places of Worship

  • Mosques: Al-Rashid (historic first mosque in Canada, now at Fort Edmonton Park; modern congregation continues), Muslim Community of Edmonton Mosque, many others across the city (Mill Woods has a high concentration)
  • Gurdwaras: Multiple Sikh temples including in Mill Woods
  • Hindu temples: Hindu Society of Alberta, Maha Ganapathy Society
  • Christian churches of many denominations and cultural communities; synagogues; Buddhist centres
