Transit

Calgary Transit — buses + CTrain (LRT). Free CTrain rides inside the downtown free-fare zone. MyFare app available.

Groceries & Ethnic Markets

African

  • African Choice Market — Three locations:
    • NE / Warehouse: 12, 2010 30 Ave NE
    • NW: 112, 3604 52 Ave NW
    • Redstone: 1120, 235 Red Embers Way NE
  • De Chosen African Market (multiple locations), Gods Glory African & West Indian Market, TK’s Authentic African Products, One Stop African Store, Safari Outpost (South African)

Asian & International

  • T&T Supermarket (Pacific Place and other locations)
  • Chalo FreshCo (South Asian), A-Mart (Korean), Basha Foods, International Fresh Foods, and many South Asian / Filipino / Middle Eastern stores

Banks (Newcomer Packages)

RBC, TD, Scotiabank, BMO, CIBC all offer dedicated newcomer packages. ATB Financial and Servus Credit Union are Alberta-based options. Downtown branches process newcomer accounts frequently.

Map: Downtown Banks

Housing

  • Calgary Housing Company — Subsidized housing and rent assistance programs
  • Private market: Rentals.ca, RentFaster, Kijiji, etc.
  • CCIS and other settlement agencies assist eligible newcomers (especially refugees) with housing search

Schools

  • Calgary Board of Education (CBE)
  • Calgary Catholic School District (CCSD)
  • Register online or at a school; bring immigration documents, proof of age, and address proof
  • Language assessment centres (e.g. CLARC) for English placement

Places of Worship

  • Mosques: Baitun Nur Mosque (one of Canada’s largest), many others especially in the NE; Saddletowne and other community mosques
  • Gurdwaras: Dashmesh Culture Centre and others (langar available)
  • Hindu temples: Calgary Hindu Temple, Swaminarayan Mandir, and others
  • Christian churches of many traditions and cultural communities; synagogues; Buddhist centres
Map: Mosques Map: Temples & Gurdwaras

Parks & Recreation

Fish Creek Provincial Park (large urban park), Bow River pathways, neighbourhood parks, recreation centres.
